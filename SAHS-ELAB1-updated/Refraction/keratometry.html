Bausch & Lomb Procedure Guide

1. Use Joystick to position central reticle inside the main circle.
2. Turn Focus Knob until the doubled bottom-right circle merges into a single sharp circle (Scheiner disc principle).
3. Turn Axis Barrel so outer horizontal plus ($+$) spur lines form a continuous straight line without scissoring.
4. Turn Horizontal Drum to superimpose the outer plus ($+$) signs in the left gap.
5. Turn Vertical Drum to superimpose the outer minus ($-$) signs in the top gap (pointed to by the red arrow).

About Keratometry Lab

Refraction & Contact Lens Unit

This clinical simulation models standard Bausch & Lomb one-position manual keratometry. It replicates Scheiner disc aperture focusing, image doubling prism displacement, astigmatic meridian scissoring, and radius of curvature conversion ($r = 337.5 / K$).

Clinical References (AAO / EyeWiki)

Keratometry Guidelines

1. Corneal Refractive Index ($n$): Manual keratometers utilize $n = 1.3375$ to account for both anterior and posterior corneal surfaces.

2. Radius Formula: Radius $r \text{ (mm)} = 337.5 / K \text{ (Diopters)}$. E.g., $45.00 \text{ D} = 7.50 \text{ mm}$.

3. Alignment Protocol: Align central crosshair. Focus double mires. Turn Axis Barrel to untwist scissored plus ($+$) spur lines. Turn Horizontal Drum to superimpose outer plus signs ($+$). Turn Vertical Drum to superimpose outer minus signs ($-$).

4. Astigmatism Rules: With-The-Rule (WTR) = vertical meridian steeper ($90^\circ \pm 30^\circ$). Against-The-Rule (ATR) = horizontal meridian steeper ($180^\circ \pm 30^\circ$).
