Neutralization Guide

1. Start with high plus power. Decrease power slowly until the Thin Sphere Lines are sharp and continuous.
2. If lines appear broken or scissored, rotate the Axis Wheel to straighten them.
3. Continue rotating the power wheel towards minus to find the Thick Cylinder Lines endpoint focus.

Lensometry Guidelines

Minus Cylinder Form Rule: Always focus the thin sphere lines first to establish the spherical power. Then rotate the power wheel toward the minus direction to bring the thick cylinder lines into focus. The difference between these two readings is the cylinder power.
