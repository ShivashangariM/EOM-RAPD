About JCC Refinement Lab

Subjective Refraction Unit

This simulator models clinical Jackson Cross-Cylinder (JCC) astigmatism refinement. It calculates vector ray-tracing and Sturm's Conoid blur circles across flip positions (Position 1 vs Position 2) to simulate true patient subjective responses for both cylinder axis ("Chase the Red") and cylinder power refinement.

Clinical JCC Rules (AAO / Optometry Guidelines)

Jackson Cross-Cylinder Rules

1. Optical Construction: A Jackson Cross-Cylinder is a combination of two cylinders of equal power but opposite signs with their axes at right angles (e.g. $+0.25\text{ DS} / -0.50\text{ DC}$ = $\pm 0.25\text{ DC}$). Spherical Equivalent = $0.00\text{ D}$.

2. Axis Refinement: Align JCC handle parallel to trial cylinder axis (dots at $45^\circ$). Rotate trial axis towards the red (minus) axis dots of the clearer position ("Chase the Red").

3. Power Refinement: Align JCC red or white dots directly over trial cylinder axis. If red dot on axis is clearer -> add $-0.25\text{ DC}$. If white dot on axis is clearer -> reduce minus cylinder power by $+0.25\text{ DC}$.

4. Spherical Equivalent Rule: When adding $-0.50\text{ DC}$ of cylinder, add $+0.25\text{ DS}$ of sphere to keep the Circle of Least Confusion on the retina.
