Understanding Clinical Refraction

Refraction is the foundation of an optometric exam — measuring how light bends through the eye's optics to find the lens power that brings a sharp, focused image onto the retina. This department covers the core instrument skills every 2nd year student must master: neutralizing spectacle lenses on a lensometer, streak retinoscopy for objective refraction, keratometry for corneal curvature, cross-cylinder refinement, and pupillary distance measurement for accurate spectacle fitting.
