About PD Measurement Lab

Refraction & Ophthalmic Dispensing Unit

This simulation models clinical pupillometry using digital corneal light reflex alignment (Victorin method / Pupillometer). It calculates monocular right (OD), monocular left (OS), total binocular (OU) pupillary distances, and accommodation-convergence shifts at working distances from infinity down to 30 cm.

Clinical References (AAO / Dispensing Guidelines)

Pupillometry Standard Rules

1. Monocular vs Binocular PD: Monocular PDs measure the distance from facial midline (nose bridge) to each corneal light reflex individually. Binocular PD = $\text{PD}_{\text{OD}} + \text{PD}_{\text{OS}}$.

2. Distance vs Near PD Shift: When fixating a near object (e.g. at 40 cm), the eyes converge. Near PD is typically **3.0 to 4.0 mm less** than Distance PD.

3. Corneal Reflex (Victorin Method): Aligning reticle hair lines with corneal light reflections (Purkinje Image I) avoids error caused by pupil asymmetry or visual axis decentration.

4. Asymmetry Significance: Asymmetric monocular PDs (e.g., OD 31.0 mm / OS 34.0 mm) must be dispensed with monocular centration to prevent unwanted induced vertical or horizontal prism.
