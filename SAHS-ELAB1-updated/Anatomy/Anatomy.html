Foundations: Ocular Anatomy & Instrumentation

The Anatomy department is the 1st-year starting point of the SAHS-E-LAB curriculum — before students touch a phoropter or retinoscope, they need to understand the structures of the eye and the clinical instrument every exam begins with: the slit lamp biomicroscope. This module builds that foundation through hands-on illumination-technique practice, not just diagrams.
