Quick Reference

• Direct focal / optic section: narrow slit at 45–60°, localizes depth in the cornea.
• Specular reflection: mirror angle to view the endothelial mosaic.
• Sclerotic scatter: beam at the limbus, cornea stays dark unless it scatters (edema).
• Retro-illumination: light bounced off the iris/fundus reveals lens & corneal opacities.
