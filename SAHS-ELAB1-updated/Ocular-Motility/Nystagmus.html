Vestibulo-Ocular Keys

Nystagmus Slow vs. Quick Corrective Phases

Jerk nystagmus consists of a slow phase drift (representing pathologically asymmetric vestibular input) and a quick phase saccadic correction back to target. By clinical convention, nystagmus is named after the direction of the **quick phase**.
