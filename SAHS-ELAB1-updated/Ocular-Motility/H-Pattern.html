Purpose of the H-Pattern

The clinical H-Pattern is utilized instead of circular paths because it isolates the recti and oblique actions. Looking fully laterally first aligns the visual axis; subsequent vertical movements isolate either the vertical recti (when eyes are abducted) or the oblique muscles (when eyes are adducted).

Clinical Pearls & Trace Diagnostics

Diagnostic landmarks of the swinging target examination (AAO, EyeWiki).

How the H-Pattern Isolates Muscles

When the eye is abducted, the Superior and Inferior Recti are the only muscles capable of vertical movement. Conversely, when the eye is adducted, the oblique muscles (SO & IO) are aligned with the visual axis and serve as the sole elevators/depressors.
