Understanding Cardinal Gaze Testing

Standard visual motor screenings examine EOM function inside the 9-points cardinal field. Under Hering's Law of Equal Innervation, yoke muscles receive equal neural impulses. For example, looking up-and-right (Dextroelevation) tests the Right Superior Rectus paired with the Left Inferior Oblique.

Academic definitions and diagnostic criteria (AAO, AOA).

Hering's Law (Equal Innervation)

States that corresponding yoke muscles in both eyes receive equal and simultaneous neural stimulation. This allows the eyes to move in a coordinated, conjugate fashion during visual pursuit.
