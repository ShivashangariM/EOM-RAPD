Beginner's Neuro-Optometric Dictionary

Hering's Law of Equal Innervation

Equal neural voltage is sent to synergistic yolk muscles of both eyes during conjugate gaze movements.

Yolk Muscle Pairings

Extraocular muscles in each eye that work together to move eyes in tandem (e.g. Right LR & Left MR).

Uncrossed vs. Crossed Diplopia

Uncrossed: Red image on same side (Esotropia). Crossed: Red image on opposite side (Exotropia).

Maximal Separation Principle

Image separation is maximum in the field of action of the paretic muscle; the false image belongs to the paretic eye.

About Hess & Diplopia Charting

The Hess Screen and 9-Gaze Diplopia Chart are complementary diagnostic tests used in neuro-ophthalmology to isolate ocular misalignment, identify paretic extraocular muscles, and record double vision separation across all 9 cardinal gaze positions.

Clinical Interpretation Reference

1. Hess Chart Principle

The smaller plotted chart indicates the affected paretic eye. The greatest inward coordinate contraction points directly to the paretic muscle.

2. Diplopia Chart Principle

Maximum separation between the false red line (OD) and real black line (OS) occurs in the field of action of the weak muscle.
