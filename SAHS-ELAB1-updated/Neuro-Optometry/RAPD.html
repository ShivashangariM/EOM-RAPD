AAO, AOA & Bell et al. Pupillary Grading Scale

American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO)

In the swinging flashlight test, light is moved briskly between eyes with a 2-3 second dwell. In a unilateral prechiasmatic afferent defect, both pupils paradoxically dilate when light arrives on the diseased eye because the perceived luminance drops.

Bell et al. Clinical RAPD Grading Protocol

Grade 1+: Initial weak constriction followed by pupillary escape.
Grade 2+: Initial stall / hesitation followed by escape.
Grade 3+: Immediate dilation without any initial constriction or hesitation.
Grade 4+: Immediate wide dilation.
Grade 5+: Immediate dilation in an amaurotic (NLP) eye.
