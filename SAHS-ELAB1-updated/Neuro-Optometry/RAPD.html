Diagnostic Reference Rules

• Marcus Gunn (RAPD): Intact consensual/resting loops. Swings onto the abnormal side provoke paradoxical escape dilation.
• CN III Palsy: Efferent parasympathetic block yields a fixed dilated pupil unresponsive to light or consensual cues.
• Horner's: Pronounced anisocoria in miosis (dark room) highlighting a prominent dilation lag.
• Argyll Robertson: Classic small, irregular bilateral pupils with Light-Near Dissociation.

Clinical Room Illumination Help

Standard pupillary light evaluations require a **Dim Room (Level 1)** to maximize the dynamic range of pupil constriction. In contrast, examining for **Horner's Syndrome** demands miosis measurements under both **Dim (exposes dilation lag)** and **Bright (minimizes anisocoria)** illumination levels. Pupil Grading Syllabus

Reference Scale
Grade 0: Normal Eye 0.0 Log Filter Balance

Equal Squeezing (Constriction to 2.5 mm)

Both pupils squeeze down perfectly to **2.5 mm** under direct or shared light. Zero expansion escape or delay.

Grade 1+: Mild Defect 0.3 Log Filter Balance

Weak Shrinking & Early Expansion (Escape)

Short initial shrinking down to **3.5 mm**, quickly followed by early "escape" expanding back to **5.0 mm**. Balances symmetrically with a **0.3 Log** filter.

Grade 2+: Moderate Defect 0.6 - 0.9 Log Filter Balance

No Initial Movement (Stall) & Late Expansion

Pupils show no response (stall at **5.5 mm**), holding position before expanding wider into a **6.5 mm** state. Balances with a **0.6 to 0.9 Log** filter.

Grade 3+: Severe Defect 1.2 - 1.5 Log Filter Balance

Immediate Direct Expansion (Dilation)

Pupils expand immediately to **6.8 mm** when light is swung to OD. Balances with a **1.2 to 1.5 Log** filter.

Grade 4+: Advanced Defect 1.8 - 2.0 Log Filter Balance

Wide Expansion with a Minor Squeeze Wave

Immediate expansion to **7.0 mm** with a subtle secondary squeeze wave down to **6.3 mm**. Balances with a **1.8 to 2.0 Log** filter.

Grade 5+: Blind Eye (NLP) Unbalanced (Infinity Log)

Zero Direct Response / Fully Expanded

Absolute sensory blockage. Direct light feels exactly like darkness. Pupils stay fully expanded at **7.5 mm**.

AAO, AOA, and academic pupillary diagnostic criteria.

Relative Afferent Pupillary Defect (RAPD)

Identified via the **Swinging Flashlight Test**. Light shined in the diseased eye yields less constriction (paradoxical dilation) because the brain perceives a net decrease in afferent input compared to stimulation of the normal fellow eye.
