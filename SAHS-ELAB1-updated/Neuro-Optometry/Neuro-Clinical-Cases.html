Clinical Cases Syllabus Guide

Neuro-optometric case examinations analyze visual pathways connecting retina phototransducers to downstream brainstem nuclei.

• Oculomotor CN III Nerve: Controls internal iris sphincters (constriction) and vertical/adduction motors. Compression yields fixed dilation, complete ptosis, and divergent "down-and-out" deviation.

• Abducens CN VI Nerve: Directly innervates the Lateral Rectus. Paralysis yields esotropia (inward turn) and horizontal lateral sweep failure.

• Neuromuscular Junctions (Myasthenia): Dynamic post-synaptic autoantibodies produce fatigable eyelid droops. Reversible through localized cooling (Ice Pack test).
