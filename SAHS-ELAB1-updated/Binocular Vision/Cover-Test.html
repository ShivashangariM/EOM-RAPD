Monocular Cover-Uncover vs. Alternate Cover Tests

The monocular cover test identifies heterotropias (manifest strabismus) by observing if the uncovered eye makes a corrective saccade to take up foveal fixation. The alternate cover test breaks binocular fusion completely, revealing heterophorias (latent deviations) as the eye under the cover drifts.

AAO (Sensory & Motor Loops)

A monocular cover test isolates the fixating eye to reveal manifest deviations (strabismus). The alternate cover test completely breaks binocular fusion by preventing shared light projection, isolating latent phoric deviations.

AOA Clinical Standards

prismatic neutralization must occur symmetrically to reduce EOM target drift completely. Align BI prisms for exodeviations, BO prisms for esodeviations, BD prisms for hyperdeviations, and BU prisms for hypodeviations.
