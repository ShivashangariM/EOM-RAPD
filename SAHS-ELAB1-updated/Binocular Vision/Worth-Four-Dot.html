Worth Four Dot Fundamentals

The Worth Four Dot (W4D) test assesses flat fusion, suppression, and anomalous retinal correspondence (ARC). It utilizes red/green anaglyph glasses to dissociate the eyes visually, projecting 1 red, 2 green, and 1 white dot.

AAO (Sensory Fusion)

The Worth Four Dot test uses color-dissociation filters (traditionally Red OD / Green OS) to determine if a patient has normal flat sensory fusion, suppression of one eye, or diplopia.

EyeWiki Diagnostic Criteria

When interpreting results, remember the color filter rules: the red lens only permits visualization of the red (top) and white (bottom) dots. The green lens permits visualization of the green (side) and white (bottom) dots.
