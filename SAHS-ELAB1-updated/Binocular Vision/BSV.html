BSV Keys

Worth's Three Levels of BSV

1. Simultaneous Macular Perception (Level 1): Ability to perceive two dissimilar images at once.
2. Flat Fusion (Level 2): Ability to merge similar images with check elements into a single 2D picture.
3. Stereopsis (Level 3): Ability to resolve subtle horizontal retinal disparity as three-dimensional depth.

AAO (Sensory & Motor BSV Scales)

Binocular single vision requires simultaneous retinal projections converging onto matched corresponding cortical receptive fields. This occurs at three hierarchical levels, reaching a fine stereoacuity threshold of 40 seconds of arc in normal healthy individuals.
