Slit Lamp, Gonioscopy & 90D Principles

Clinical Guidelines (AAO / BCLA / EyeWiki)

1. Optical Section: Uses a 0.1mm-0.5mm beam at 45°-60° to slice through transparent ocular tissues, separating corneal epithelium, stroma, endothelium, and the crystalline lens.

2. Specular Reflection: Achieved when the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence (~35°), revealing the endothelial cell mosaic at 25x-40x magnification.

3. Gonioscopy Anatomy: A contact gonioprism neutralizes corneal curvature to overcome total internal reflection. Landmarks from posterior to anterior: Iris root → Ciliary Body Band → Scleral Spur → Trabecular Meshwork → Schwalbe's Line.

4. 90D Funduscopy: Forms an inverted, real stereoscopic aerial image of the retina and optic nerve head, enabling cup-to-disc ratio and neuroretinal rim assessment.
